# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX/Design Lead drives user-centered design practices throughout the project lifecycle. They conduct user research, establish design standards, and ensure usability and accessibility are prioritized alongside functionality.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research and discovery activities to validate assumptions
- Define design standards, patterns, and interaction guidelines
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user needs and requirements
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Participate in design and usability reviews
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback
- Support QA team in creating usability test plans

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered solutions that meet customer needs
- Establish consistent, scalable design systems
- Reduce rework by catching usability issues early
- Improve user satisfaction and adoption rates

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and critiques
- User research findings and reports
- Design specification documents and style guides
- Collaboration with Developers on implementation details
- Usability testing sessions and feedback synthesis

### Interactions
- Works closely with **Product Managers** on user requirements and priorities
- Partners with **Developers** to ensure designs are implementable and to guide front-end development
- Collaborates with **QA Lead** on usability and accessibility testing
- Reports to **Project Manager** on timeline impacts and design dependencies

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager orchestrates the planning, coordination, and execution of releases. They manage release schedules, coordinate with all teams, mitigate deployment risks, and ensure clear communication with stakeholders throughout the release cycle.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases aligned with business and product goals
- Coordinate with Developers, QA, and other teams on release readiness
- Manage release checklists, go/no-go decisions, and sign-offs
- Oversee deployment procedures and verify post-deployment health
- Manage rollback procedures if issues arise post-deployment
- Coordinate release notes, communications, and stakeholder notifications
- Track and manage release-related risks and blockers
- Maintain release calendar and documentation

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal risk and disruption
- Ensure clear visibility into release status for all stakeholders
- Reduce deployment-related incidents and outages
- Enable faster, more frequent releases through reliable processes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and status updates
- Pre-release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Post-release verification reports and incident notifications
- Coordination with on-call teams for production support

### Interactions
- Partners with **Project Managers** on release scheduling and milestone alignment
- Works with **Developers** on deployment readiness and technical integration
- Collaborates with **QA Lead** on test completion and release signoff
- Communicates with **Product Managers** on release scope and customer impact
- Coordinates with **Security & Compliance Lead** on security validation before release
- Reports to **Project Sponsor** on release outcomes and business metrics

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security & Compliance Lead integrates security and compliance practices throughout the project lifecycle. They identify risks, guide secure development practices, coordinate security reviews, and ensure adherence to regulatory and organizational requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess security risks early in the project planning phase
- Define security requirements and standards for the project
- Conduct and coordinate security reviews of designs, code, and infrastructure
- Provide guidance to Developers on secure coding practices and frameworks
- Plan and execute security testing (threat modeling, penetration testing, etc.)
- Manage security vulnerabilities and coordinate remediation efforts
- Ensure compliance with relevant regulations, standards, and organizational policies
- Maintain security documentation and incident response procedures
- Escalate security issues and track resolution

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant solutions that protect customer data and systems
- Build security awareness and practices into team culture
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Enable rapid response to security incidents

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement discussions and design reviews
- Security testing plans and vulnerability reports
- Compliance audits and regulatory assessment results
- Incident response coordination and post-incident analysis
- Security guidance and team training

### Interactions
- Partners with **Developers** to provide secure development guidance and code review insights
- Collaborates with **QA Lead** on security and penetration testing planning
- Works with **Project Managers** on security dependencies and risk mitigation
- Advises **Product Managers** on security implications of feature requests
- Supports **Release Manager** with pre-release security validation
- Escalates critical security incidents to **Project Sponsor** and appropriate leadership

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead owns the quality strategy for the project. They define testing approaches, establish quality standards, coordinate testing activities, and ensure acceptance criteria are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the testing strategy and quality standards for the project
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Manage test environments and test data
- Conduct quality reviews and identify defects early
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition with Product and Development teams
- Plan and execute different testing types (unit, integration, e2e, performance, security, usability)
- Manage test coverage and quality metrics
- Ensure Definition of Done criteria are met before work is marked complete

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality solutions that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects and rework through early quality validation
- Maintain consistent quality standards across releases
- Improve customer satisfaction and reduce support incidents

### Typical Communication
- Test plan and test case documentation
- Quality status reports and defect tracking
- Testing milestone reviews and sign-offs
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria and test approach
- Quality metrics and trend reporting

### Interactions
- Partners with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and success metrics definition
- Works with **Developers** on testing strategy and Definition of Done
- Collaborates with **UX/Design Lead** on usability and accessibility testing
- Supports **Release Manager** with quality readiness sign-off
- Reports to **Project Manager** on quality status and risks
- Communicates with **Security & Compliance Lead** on security and compliance testing
